# Image credits & sourcing policy

All photography on this prototype is **real, non-AI stock photography sourced from Pexels** (free licence).
Per project direction: **no AI-generated imagery** is used anywhere on the site, and search results that
originated from AI-heavy sources (Vecteezy, Pinterest, Freepik, etc.) were reviewed and **excluded**.
One search result was explicitly labelled "AI Generated" (Vecteezy, an afro-hair portrait) and was discarded.

Images are used as downloaded and should be replaced with licensed, consented East African client
photography before production launch.

---

Reference design inspected: `careo-master.zip` (Labspa/Careo HTML theme). Its tokens are carried into
`styles.css`: Poppins and Lato typography, `#bc1939` wine accent, `#1a1824` dark, `#1d1b28` heading ink,
`#666666` body, `#fff` white, `#f9f9f9` light silver, 90px/75px section spacing, 10px/30px corner radii.
The source did not include a formal design-token file; values were extracted from `css/style.css` and
responsive overrides. Decorative shapes are limited to circles and lines — no diamond motifs are used
anywhere in the UI.
